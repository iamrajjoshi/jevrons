Jevrons

A small network in which every neuron is a live call to Jev. A hidden neuron sends Jev the products x·w of its nonzero pixels, plus its bias, and asks whether they add up to more than zero. Jev's probability is the activation. The sum is also computed here, so every answer can be checked: pink marks a neuron where Jev and the arithmetic disagree.

inputdraw a digit

What the network sees: 28×28, digit fit into a 20×20 box and centered by mass, as in MNIST.
